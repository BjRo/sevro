consulting are permitted.

Charging for copies, licensing, or hosted access to Sevro's functionality,

disguise a charge for the runner. Retain copyright, license, origin, and
modification notices. This is source-available, not an OSI open-source license.

[Contribution terms](../CONTRIBUTING.md#contribution-licensing) give recipients
the public license and grant Björn Rochel additional copyright relicensing
rights. Contributors keep copyright. Other recipients do not automatically
receive those broader rights. The project describes its current free terms;
it does not promise that alternative licenses will never be offered.

## Earlier releases

Published `0.1.0-rc.1` and `0.1.0-rc.2` retain BUSL-1.1, their original
production grant, and their per-version change date. Each changes to MPL-2.0
two years after its first public availability. This checkout does not revoke
or rewrite earlier grants. The [retained rc.2 license](licenses/BUSL-1.1-0.1.0-rc.2.txt)
records those terms.

Package metadata from `0.1.0-rc.3` uses `SEE LICENSE IN LICENSE`: the new license
has no SPDX identifier. The matching LICENSE travels in the tarball. These
terms do not alter the already published `rc.1` or `rc.2` artifacts.

## Branding and independently authored material

[Brand permissions](assets/README.md) are separate. Björn Rochel created the
logo; Pierce Brown's _Red Rising_ inspired the name. [Acknowledgements](acknowledgements.md)
credit that inspiration without implying affiliation or ownership.

The runner license does not automatically govern independently authored cases,
datasets, extensions, prompts, or outputs. Their authors/input licenses
determine those rights. Copies of covered Sevro code remain covered.
