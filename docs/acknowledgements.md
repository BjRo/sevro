# Acknowledgements

Sevro is named after the character in [Pierce Brown's _Red Rising_ series](https://www.piercebrown.com/redrisingsaga).
Thank you, Pierce Brown, for the world and characters that inspired both Sevro
and Darrow. This acknowledges inspiration, not ownership of the fictional
character or affiliation with the author.

Björn Rochel created the Sevro logo. Its black/red track motif gives the runner
its own visual identity alongside [Darrow](https://github.com/BjRo/darrow).
Read [brand assets and permitted uses](assets/README.md).

Sevro's standalone runner grew out of Darrow's evaluation work. Darrow remains
an integration and source of ideas; the deterministic example works independently.
